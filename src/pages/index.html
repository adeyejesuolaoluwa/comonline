The story

A diagnosis explains.
A story helps us
understand.

For years, P.J. Ray lived with something he could not name. A breakdown brought a diagnosis of bipolar disorder, and a different understanding of the life that came before.

Living with Bipolar looks back at the chaos, the impact on other people, and the work of rebuilding. It is a personal account of an experience that can be hard to put into words.
