What is Living with Bipolar about?+

It is a memoir by P.J. Ray about years of living with undiagnosed bipolar disorder, a breakdown, a diagnosis, and the personal journey toward recovery.

Explore the story ↗
Who is P.J. Ray?+

P.J. Ray is the author of the memoir. Born in Berkshire, he spent 25 years working as a chef before moving into logistics and later writing.
