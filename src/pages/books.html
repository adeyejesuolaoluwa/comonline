Between the covers

Putting a life
into words.

He never knew he had bipolar; it’s something he had lived with for years, before having a breakdown and then being diagnosed with it.

It had been slowly destroying his life. Along the way it had hurt other people, not intentionally, but it just did. It’s like having a self-destruction button.

Living with Bipolar is P.J. Ray’s account of that experience: the years before diagnosis, its impact on a life and the people around it, and the personal journey toward recovery.
