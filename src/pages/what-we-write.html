Understanding
starts with
a story.

Not a textbook. Not a diagnosis.
A personal account of living with bipolar disorder.

01

Before
the name.

Living without an explanation

For years, the symptoms were there, but the diagnosis was not. The memoir looks back at a life shaped by an illness the author did not yet know he had.

02

More than
one life.

The people around us

A personal struggle rarely stays personal. Ray reflects on the impact on other people, the harm that was never intended, and the difficulty of making sense of what happened.

03

Finding
a way forward.

Diagnosis and recovery

A breakdown brought a diagnosis, and with it a different way of understanding the past. The story follows the personal and emotional work of rebuilding.

The reason for sharing

To put experience into words.
To make room for understanding.

This is one person’s story, rather than medical advice. Each experience of bipolar disorder is different.
