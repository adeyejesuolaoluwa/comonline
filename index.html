About the book

A memoir about survival and self-discovery.

Living with Bipolar shares P.J. Ray’s journey through years of unrecognized symptoms, a life-changing breakdown, and the process of understanding and healing.

Authentic story

Ray writes with raw honesty about the chaos, regret, and hope of living with bipolar disorder.

Personal recovery

The memoir explores diagnosis, treatment, and the emotional work needed to move forward.

Deep empathy

It shows how bipolar disorder affects not just one life, but the people around it.

About the author

P.J. Ray

P.J. Ray writes from lived experience and uses their memoir to explain how bipolar disorder shaped a life before diagnosis and after recovery.

The book is an invitation to readers who want an honest portrait of mental health, self-destruction, and the work it takes to rebuild.
