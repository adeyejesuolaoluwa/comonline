P.J. Ray

P.J. Ray is the author of Living with Bipolar, a memoir that shares years of unrecognized symptoms, a life-changing diagnosis, and the emotional path back to stability.

Author portrait P.J. Ray

About the author

Born in the Berkshire countryside, I went on to study in Oxford before continuing my education at Reading University. My professional journey began early, starting at the age of 12 in the family business, where I developed a strong work ethic and practical skills that would shape my career.

I spent 25 years working as a chef, gaining extensive experience across the UK and during three years living and working in Gibraltar. This chapter of my life allowed me to refine my discipline, creativity, and resilience in fast-paced environments.

Following my time in the culinary industry, I transitioned into logistics, where I founded and managed my own External Transport Manager business. This venture expanded my expertise in operations, compliance, and business management.

Today, I have embraced a new path as an author, bringing together the depth of my life experiences, diverse career background, and passion for storytelling into my writing.
