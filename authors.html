Author

P.J. Ray

P.J. Ray is the author of Living with Bipolar, a memoir that shares years of unrecognized symptoms, a life-changing diagnosis, and the emotional path back to stability.

About P.J. Ray

P.J. Ray writes from personal experience, giving readers an honest, compassionate look at mental health and the challenges of living with bipolar disorder.

Their memoir explores how the condition slowly damaged life and relationships before the truth was finally named.
