Living with Bipolar

P.J. Ray • Paperback • 257 pages

Book summary

He never knew he had bipolar; it was something he had lived with for years before a breakdown led to diagnosis. It had been slowly destroying his life and hurting the people around him, often without intention. This is a raw, honest reflection on living with bipolar disorder and the pain it caused others, which he now has to live with.
