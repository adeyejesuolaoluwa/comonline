What We Write

The writing behind the memoir

This site represents a single memoir and its related writing: personal reflection, mental health insight, and the story of living with bipolar disorder.

Memoir content

First-person storytelling about learning to live with bipolar disorder, rather than simply recovering from it.

Mental health insight

Raw, honest reflection on living with bipolar disorder and the pain it caused others, which I now have to live with.

Author reflection

Personal writing that is honest, grounded, and all good in its intent to make meaning from the past.

Read the story

Living with Bipolar is written to help readers understand life with a hidden disorder and the work of reclaiming hope.
